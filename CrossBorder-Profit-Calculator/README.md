CrossBorder Profit Calculator

AI-powered cross-border e-commerce profit analysis tool.

跨境电商利润分析工具，帮助卖家快速计算产品成本、平台费用、利润率，并提供 AI 优化建议。

🌟 Features
1. Profit Calculation
- Calculate product revenue
- Calculate total cost
- Calculate net profit
- Calculate profit margin

2. Platform Fee Analysis
Support analysis of:
- Product cost
- International shipping
- Platform commission
- Advertising cost
- Other expenses

3. Target Price Calculation
Calculate recommended selling price based on target profit margin.

4. AI Profit Analysis
Generate business suggestions based on calculation results:

- Profit status evaluation
- Cost structure analysis
- Optimization suggestions
- Recommended price range
